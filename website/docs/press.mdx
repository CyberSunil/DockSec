## Writing about DockSec?

The material most people want:

- **The pitch in one line** - DockSec tells you which container findings
  actually matter and what to do about them, running entirely on your own
  infrastructure.
- **A reproducible demo** - `docksec -i node:18 --image-only --scan-only`
  produces 2,200 findings and ranks 9 as `Fix Now`. The full write-up is in the
  [node:18 case study](./case-studies/node-18).
- **What it does not do** - the [limitations page](./limitations) is
  deliberately blunt, and we would rather be quoted from it than have the gaps
  discovered later.
- **Logos and assets** - in the
  [repository](https://github.com/OWASP/DockSec/tree/main/images).

For anything else, [open an issue](https://github.com/OWASP/DockSec/issues) or
find the project on the OWASP Slack at `#project-docksec`.
